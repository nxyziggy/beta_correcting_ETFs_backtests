# Extension through 2 November 2026

This specification is recorded before viewing extension returns. The existing 3-7 holdings selection, Q75 primary and Q50 sensitivity, Q25 exit, one-quarter qualification, minimum-fill exception, costs, weights, universe and financial lags are unchanged.

The final complete month is October 2026. Its calendar label is 31 October; the observed trading close is 30 October. The terminal daily valuation uses actual 2 November closes. No after-hours quotes or invented weekend prices are used.

All previously captured raw sources and all portfolio calculations through 31 August are preserved. For each instrument, the extension chains dated adjusted-close ratios from the newly captured 31 August anchor, using one current source vintage for each ratio. This accommodates later corporate-action adjustments without splicing incompatible adjusted-price levels or rewriting old returns. The old monthly-price sensitivity keeps its historical treatment through August; every variant uses dated daily observations for the extension. Current Digrin monthly snapshots are captured solely as an independent quality check because some labels or values may be stale.

Annual overviews for all 30 still-listed candidate companies and full quarterly financial statements for the original nine-company quarterly subset are reviewed. Only newer financial periods are appended; revised values for previously captured periods are logged and retained as evidence rather than backdated. New September-period reports remain ineligible for September/October trades under the existing 90/180-day lags and first-of-month information cutoff. Already captured reports can become eligible as time advances.

FX uses observed Federal Reserve H.10 daily observations through 30 October. The Federal Reserve has not yet published 2 November observations, so the terminal valuation uses that day's ECB reference rates. CHF/USD, DKK/USD and NOK/USD are calculated from the published ECB EUR crosses. This terminal source change is explicit; an additional terminal valuation using the latest Federal Reserve rates is reported as a sensitivity. Both are valuations from observed reference rates, not executable FX quotations.

Besides monthly and quarterly ledgers, the update reports daily valuations from September through 2 November using the positions actually selected at each monthly rebalance. The daily extension does not add daily trading. On non-trading calendar month ends, the dated last trading close is mapped to the calendar portfolio label with observation dates retained. Daily risk statistics for the entire historical strategy are not inferred from this short extension.

The September delivery remains the historical baseline. Comparisons with its terminal return explicitly have different end dates. Historical preservation is tested on identical complete-month dates. No performance-based rule or universe change is authorized by this extension.
