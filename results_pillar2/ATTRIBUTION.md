# Why the US and EU selection results differ

Any prior public support screen, direct/subsidiary definition; 2019-06-30–2026-11-02. The same accounting screen produces different exposure and timing in two different company pools.

## US

Bloom’s additive profit contributions are EQUAL +148.44pp, Q50 -18.73pp, Q75 -22.06pp. Its first holding dates are EQUAL 2019-06-30, Q50 2026-09-30, Q75 2026-09-30. The largest negative US Q75/control differences are BE (-170.50pp), INTC (-53.57pp), FSLR (-51.84pp). These numbers describe the existing accounting strategy, not the alternative trend or momentum scores.

## EU

The score is based on reported operating profitability and cash earnings quality, not a forecast of the next share-price recovery. A stock can appreciate before those accounts improve. Concentration makes those selection and timing differences larger. These results do not establish a durable geographic advantage for the rule.

## Accounting and interpretation

For each stock: terminal value plus delisting cash proceeds minus net purchases minus its trading costs. Starting NAV is 100, so one NAV unit is one return percentage point. Euro portfolios include the final currency conversion; EDF delisting proceeds are included explicitly. Contributions sum to each portfolio return, and their differences sum to each strategy/control gap.

These are additive profit contributions along the actual portfolio paths. They include allocation, entry/exit timing and the effect of different portfolio wealth over time. They are not isolated causal effects or the result of rerunning without a stock. Average weights include zero exposure in months when a stock was not held and include initial deployment.
